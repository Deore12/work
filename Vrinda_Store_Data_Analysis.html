💡 Business Conclusions
🎯
Target audience: Women aged 18–59 are the core customer base — all marketing and product decisions should center this segment.
📱
Focus platforms: Amazon, Myntra, and Flipkart together handle 80%+ of sales — advertising budget should be concentrated on these three channels.
📍
Top geography: Maharashtra and Karnataka are the highest-revenue states — ideal for targeted regional promotions and faster logistics.
👘
Best product lines: Sets and Kurtas dominate at 73.5% of orders — inventory planning and new launches should prioritize these categories.
✅
Fulfillment is strong at 92.2% delivery rate, but the 3.4% return rate should be investigated to identify size/category patterns driving returns.
📅
Q1 (Jan–Mar) is the strongest quarter — festival and seasonal promotions, offers, and new stock launches should be front-loaded in this window.
