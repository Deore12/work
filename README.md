

Vrinda Store Data Analysis Project

Project Objective
The primary goal of this project is to analyze the 2022 annual sales data of Vrinda Store to understand customer behavior and identify trends. These insights are intended to help the store owner make data-driven decisions to drive sales growth for 2023. 

Tech Stack & Skills

Tools: Microsoft Excel (Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers).
Key Skills: Data Cleaning, Data Transformation, Descriptive Analysis, Dashboard Creation. 

Data Processing Steps

Data Cleaning: Handled null values, removed duplicates, and corrected inconsistent formatting (e.g., replacing 'M' with 'Men' and 'W' with 'Women').
Data Transformation: Created new attributes such as 'Age Group' (e.g., Teen, Adult, Senior) and 'Month' to enable more granular analysis.
Analysis: Used Pivot Tables to aggregate sales by gender, region, channel, and category. 

Key Business Insights

Target Audience: Women are the primary customers, accounting for approximately 64% of total purchases.
Top Regions: Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh are the top 3 contributing states, representing about 35% of total sales. 
Age Demographics: The adult age group (31-50 years) is the most significant contributor, driving roughly 50% of the sales.
Sales Channels: Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra are the dominant platforms, contributing nearly 80% of total revenue.
Peak Season: Highest sales activity was recorded in February and March. 

Final Recommendations

Targeted Marketing: Focus advertising campaigns on women aged 30-49 residing in Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh.
Channel Optimization: Prioritize inventory and promotions on high-performing online channels like Amazon and Flipkart.
